THREADLINE is an independent clothing studio making a better version of the pieces we reach for automatically.

Why THREADLINE

A closet should help you live, not ask for more attention.

We started THREADLINE with a simple observation: the pieces that matter most are usually the least dramatic. They survive the long day, the weekend plan, the laundry cycle and the changing version of your style.

Make fewer things. Make them easier to love. Leave space for the person wearing them.

Our work is about refining that reliable middle ground—between relaxed and considered, familiar and genuinely well made.

SMALL BATCH / INDIA / SINCE 2026

How we make

Small runs, thoughtful changes.

We launch in deliberately limited quantities, which lets us listen, improve and avoid making more than people want to wear. Each new piece begins with wear tests, fabric notes and the boringly important details: a neck that holds, a pocket that sits right, a hem that does not curl.

Care & repair

Use is part of the design.

Good cotton softens. Twill gains character. If a favourite piece needs attention, we want you to be able to keep it in rotation rather than replace it.
